# Kart Clash

## Platform
Desktop keyboard browser game, local single player with explicitly labelled bots. Smaller screens support menus, not touch driving.

## Product truth
The user's attached brief authorizes a complete replacement of the active F1 experience by original cartoon kart combat. Choose appearance, play a three-minute free-for-all, collect six items, respawn, see results and replay. Seven steering challenges and a separate AI Academy share simulation infrastructure. GPU training is optional and starts only by explicit user action. No online multiplayer claim.

## Constraints
Reuse p5.Vector and protected Vehicle locomotion. Every physical entity inherits Vehicle. Keep old examples, laboratories and racing models. Original procedural 3D geometry and synthesized audio. Local static frontend; optional loopback Academy service. No external publication.

## Stack
Existing plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript, p5.js and locally bundled Three.js, Node evaluation of the identical simulation, optional existing Python/PyTorch CUDA environment. Routine choices delegated by the user's brief. French UI. Arrow-key driving, Space item, Escape pause, C camera, plain D inspection. Visual lobby and three original character choices.
